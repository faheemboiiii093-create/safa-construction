SAFA CONCIERGE — PROJECT FAQ

A clearer first
conversation.

These answers use only information published on this page. No AI assistant, live chat, or enquiry service is connected.

Can I get a construction estimate here?

The calculator uses clearly labelled demonstration rates, not official SAFA CONSTRUCTION prices or a quotation. A project-specific estimate needs verified rates, approved scope, drawings, and site information.

Can SAFA generate an AI home design or image?

This site can prepare a preliminary browser-only planning brief. No AI provider or image-generation API is configured, so it does not generate images or architectural plans.

Can I view my construction progress online?

Private project records, customer accounts, progress photographs, and approved documents are not connected. This page does not show live project data.

Are the portfolio images completed SAFA projects?

No. The gallery is labelled as illustrative concept photography. No completed SAFA project portfolio or before-and-after records have been supplied to this website.

How can I discuss a project?

Use the enquiry form to prepare a draft on your device. The company’s phone, email, and WhatsApp details are not configured, so the form does not send or store your information.
